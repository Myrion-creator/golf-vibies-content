You know which way your bad shot goes. Use it.

If eight out of ten of your misses curve right, aiming down the middle means half the fairway is useless to you. Aim down the left edge instead and your normal shot finds grass, while your good one finds the middle. You haven't fixed anything — you've stopped being punished for it.

This feels like giving up. It isn't. It's the difference between playing the course with the swing you have and playing it with the swing you wish you had.

Fix the slice on the range. Use it on the course.
