If you're trying to lift the ball into the air, you'll keep hitting it thin.

An iron is built to hit down. The clubface strikes the ball first, keeps travelling downward, and takes the turf after it. The loft does the lifting — not you. Try to help it up and the club bottoms out behind the ball, and you either hit it fat or catch it thin.

The drill:
Put a tee in the ground an inch in front of your ball. Hit the ball and clip the tee out. If you're hitting up, you'll never reach it.

Ball first, turf second. Every good iron shot in history.
