The fairway bunker is a lie problem, not a power problem.

You need to catch the ball before the sand, which means you cannot shift down into it the way you would from turf. So you stand a little taller and swing a little quieter — and a quieter swing with the same club comes up short.

The setup:
→ One or two more clubs than the yardage
→ Grip down an inch for control
→ Feet barely dug in, weight steady
→ Swing at 80 percent, ball first

And check the lip before you choose. No club carries a wall.
