That 150-metre 7-iron on the range is not a 150-metre 7-iron on the course.

Range balls are built to fly shorter and take a beating, and after a few thousand hits they're scuffed, out of round and soft. Off the same swing they come up short of a premium ball, and they spin differently out of every lie.

So you build a yardage book on the range, take it to the course, and come up short all day wondering what changed.

Get your numbers from the course, or from a launch monitor with your own ball. Never from a bucket.
