Nobody practises the putt that actually costs them the hole.

On the practice green, most people roll long putts because long putts are satisfying. But you don't three-putt because of the first putt alone — you three-putt because you missed the short one that followed. And the short ones are the putts you'll face four or five times a round.

Build the habit:
→ Ten putts from three feet, around the hole like a clock
→ Miss one, start the ten again
→ Only then roll anything long

Hole ten in a row and the fourth one on the course stops feeling like a test.
