Around the green, pick the shot with the fewest ways to fail.

Every extra bit of air you put under the ball adds a way to go wrong. The ladder goes: putt, then bump-and-run, then pitch, then — only if there is genuinely no other option — the flop.

Most amateurs start at the top of that list because the flop looks like golf on television. It's the shot with the smallest margin in the bag, played by people who practise it for a living.

Ask one question: what's the least amount of air that gets this job done?
