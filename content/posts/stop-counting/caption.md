The fastest way to ruin a good round is to work out what you're on.

The moment you calculate, you stop playing the shot in front of you and start protecting a number. Protecting a number makes you steer, and steering is how good rounds collapse on the sixteenth.

Try this: don't total the card until the eighteenth green. Write the numbers down, add nothing up. If someone asks where you stand, say you don't know.

It sounds like a trick. It is. It works because the only shot you can influence is the next one.
