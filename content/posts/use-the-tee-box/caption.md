You get to choose where you stand on the tee, and almost nobody uses it.

The tee box is wider than people play it. If there's water down the right, tee up on the right side and aim away from it — that angle points your ball at the safe part of the hole. Teeing up on the left and aiming right does the opposite: it aims you straight at the thing you're afraid of.

Simple rule: tee up on the same side as the trouble, then play away from it.

It costs nothing, takes five seconds, and you can do it before you've even swung.
