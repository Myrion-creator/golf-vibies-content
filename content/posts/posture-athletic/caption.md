Rigid posture is the quickest way to stop your body turning.

Golfers are told to stand up straight and often lock themselves in place: knees stiff, back arched, shoulders pulled back. Then they try to rotate and nothing moves, so the arms take over.

Try the opposite reference. Stand like you're about to catch a ball thrown at your chest: knees soft, weight in the middle of your feet, chest over your toes, arms hanging. That's an athletic position, and it rotates.

If someone nudged you, you should be able to stay balanced. If you'd topple, you're not set up to swing.
