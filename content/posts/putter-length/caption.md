Standard putter length is a shop decision, not a fitting decision.

If the putter is too long, you stand too far from the ball, your eyes sit inside the target line and you see the line differently than it is. You then compensate with your hands — and hands are what you're trying to take out of putting.

The check: take your putting stance, hang a ball from the bridge of your nose and drop it. It should land on or just inside the ball. If it lands well inside, the putter is long for you.

Shortening a putter costs almost nothing and changes what you see.
