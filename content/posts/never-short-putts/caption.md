Obvious, and still the most common miss in golf.

Leaving it short feels safe — you avoid the three-footer coming back. But a ball that stops before the hole has a zero percent chance, while one rolling at the right pace can still catch an edge and drop. Over a round that difference is worth real strokes.

The target isn't the hole. It's about 30 to 40 centimetres past it. Roll every putt as though the hole were a step further away and the short ones disappear.

You can't three-putt from a putt you've holed.
