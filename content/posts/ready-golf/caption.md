Slow play is almost never caused by one slow swing.

It's caused by everybody waiting. Four players standing still while one walks to their ball, then repeating it four times a hole. Over eighteen holes that's most of an hour.

Ready golf fixes it without rushing anyone:
→ Hit when you're ready and it's safe
→ Walk to your ball while others play
→ Take your glove off on the way to the green, not standing on it
→ Park the trolley on the side towards the next tee

Nobody has to swing faster. The gaps just disappear.
