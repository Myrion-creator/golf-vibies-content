If the ball moves around in your stance, your low point moves with it.

Where the ball sits decides whether you catch it before the ground or after. Guess it, and you'll hit one fat and the next thin with the same swing — then go looking for a fault that isn't there.

A workable map:
→ Wedges: centre
→ Mid irons: one ball forward of centre
→ Long irons and hybrids: two forward
→ Driver: inside your lead heel

Put a club on the ground at the range and check it. Most people are further back than they think with the driver and further forward with wedges.
