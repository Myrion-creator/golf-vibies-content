A putt that misses below the hole never had a chance.

Gravity works on the ball the whole way to the hole. A putt that starts above the line keeps falling toward it and can still drop in at the last moment. A putt below the line is moving away from the hole from the first metre. Golfers call it the amateur side for a reason.

So when you're unsure how much break to play, play more, not less. Err high.
