Every golfer has lost a hole trying to rescue it.

You're behind a tree, there's a gap you reckon you could thread, and the green is reachable if it comes off. It comes off maybe one time in six. The other five, the ball hits wood, moves ten metres, and you're playing the same shot again — only worse.

The chip-out costs you a stroke and guarantees the next one is from grass. The hero shot risks three and guarantees nothing.

Take the stroke. Make the putt. Walk off with bogey instead of a seven.
