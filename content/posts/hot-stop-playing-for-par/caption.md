If you shoot 95, par is not your number. Chasing it is why you shoot 95.

Par assumes two putts and a green in regulation. You hit maybe four greens a round. So on every hole you're attempting a shot that your game produces once in five tries, and paying for the other four in penalties and recovery.

Play for bogey instead. Three comfortable shots to the middle of the green, two putts, walk off. Suddenly the driver isn't compulsory, the tucked pin isn't tempting, and the blow-up holes stop happening.

Bogey golf is 90. Most people chasing par shoot 100.
