Hitting forty 7-irons in a row feels productive. It barely transfers to the course.

You never hit the same shot twice in a round. Repeating one club lets you groove a feel in that session and forget it by the next. Changing target and club every ball is harder, feels worse, and sticks far better. Researchers call it random practice; golfers call it a bad range session.

Do this instead:
→ New club and new target every single ball
→ Full routine before each one
→ Say the shot out loud before you hit it

Twenty balls like that beat eighty on autopilot.
