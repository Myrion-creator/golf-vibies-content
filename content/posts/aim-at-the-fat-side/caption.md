A flag tucked behind a bunker is a trap, and it's meant to be.

Course designers put pins near trouble precisely because they know you'll aim at them. The shot to the middle of the green leaves you a long putt. The shot at the tucked pin leaves you in sand — and sand costs you a stroke far more often than a long putt does.

The question to ask before every approach: where is the miss I can live with? Aim so that your normal miss still finds grass.

You don't have to hit it closer. You have to stop making bogeys.
