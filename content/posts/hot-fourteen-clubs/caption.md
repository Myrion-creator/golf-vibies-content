Count how many of your fourteen you actually trust. For most people it's about eight.

The 4-iron comes out twice a year and goes sideways both times. The 3-wood off the deck has never once worked. The second wedge is there because the set came with it.

Carry what you hit. Nine clubs, known distances, no deliberating. You'll play faster, think less and score the same or better — and the bag gets light enough to walk with.

A club you don't trust isn't an option. It's a liability you're carrying up every hill.
