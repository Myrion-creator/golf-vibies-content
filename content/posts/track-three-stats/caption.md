You probably don't know where your strokes actually go. Most people guess wrong.

Golfers blame the swing because the swing is what feels bad. But the strokes usually leak somewhere quieter — a chipping pattern, or a 45-minute stretch of three-putts you've forgotten by the bar.

Write three numbers on your card for three rounds:
→ Fairways hit
→ Greens in regulation
→ Total putts

That's it. Nine data points. After three rounds the weak link is obvious, and you'll stop spending range time on the thing that was already fine.
