Most amateurs have one wedge distance: full. Everything shorter is guesswork.

The fix isn't touch, it's reference points. Pick three backswing lengths and learn what each one carries. Think of your lead arm as a clock hand.

→ 9 o'clock: your short number
→ 10:30: your middle number
→ Full: your long number

Hit ten balls at each with one wedge and write down the carry. Do it for each wedge in your bag. That's an afternoon's work and a permanent set of numbers.

Guessing from 60 metres is why you're putting from twelve.
