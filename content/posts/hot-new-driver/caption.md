Every year there's a longer driver. If each one added what it claimed, the average amateur would be driving it 400 metres by now.

They aren't.

What actually moves your distance is where on the face you hit it. A centre strike with a ten-year-old head beats a heel strike with this year's model, and it isn't close. You can't buy a centre strike.

Spend it on a fitting, or on lessons, or on green fees — at least those are fun.
