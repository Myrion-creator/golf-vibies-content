Into the wind, the hardest swing is the worst swing.

A harder swing adds backspin. Backspin makes the ball climb. The wind gets underneath it, balloons it up and drops it short of where your normal swing would have put it. You swung harder and finished nearer.

Do the opposite:
→ Two more clubs than the yardage says
→ Grip down an inch
→ Ball back a touch
→ Three-quarter swing, finish low

Low and boring beats high and brave. Every time.
