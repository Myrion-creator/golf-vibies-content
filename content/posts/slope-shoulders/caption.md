Most bad shots from a slope come from standing like you're on flat ground.

On an uphill lie, your trailing shoulder drops naturally. Fight it and you'll jam the club into the hill. Let it drop, swing along the slope, and take one more club — the slope adds loft and the ball flies higher and shorter.

Downhill is the mirror image. Shoulders tilt down the hill, ball back a touch, one less club, and expect it to run.

The rule for both: swing with the hill, not against it.
