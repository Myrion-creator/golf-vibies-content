Greens change from week to week, and your hands don't automatically know it.

Rain, heat, how recently they were cut — all of it changes speed, sometimes a lot. If you walk to the first green with last week's feel, you'll spend four or five holes calibrating, and each of those holes can cost you a stroke.

Two minutes on the practice green:
→ Three putts from about ten metres, no target. Just feel the pace.
→ Three from three feet to feel the strike.

Don't try to hole anything. You're measuring, not practising.
