If you decelerate to take distance off a pitch, you'll hit it fat.

Shorter shots need a shorter swing, not a softer one. When players try to control distance with their hands instead, they take a full backswing and then slow down through the ball. The club bottoms out early, catches turf first, and the shot goes nowhere.

Keep the through-swing committed and change only the length going back. Same tempo, shorter arc, accelerate through every time.

A short confident swing beats a long frightened one.
