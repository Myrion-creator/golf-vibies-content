From just off the green, reach for the putter more often.

A chip has two ways to go wrong — you can catch it fat or thin, and you still have to judge the roll. A putt has one. Even a badly struck putt from the fringe usually finishes within a few feet. A badly struck chip can finish anywhere.

The rule: if the grass between you and the green is short enough to roll a ball through, putt it.

Tour players chip because they can. You score by removing the shot that can cost you three.
