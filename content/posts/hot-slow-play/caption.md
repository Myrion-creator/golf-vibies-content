The group ahead isn't why your round took five hours.

Watch yourself for eighteen holes. Count the seconds between reaching your ball and starting the swing. Count the practice swings. Count how long you stand over a putt you read two minutes ago.

Four players doing that, eighteen times over, is an extra hour. None of it happened on the tee you were waiting at.

Slow play is a group of individuals, every one of them convinced they're the quick one.

Be ready when it's your turn. The group behind will never learn your name, but they'll feel you.
