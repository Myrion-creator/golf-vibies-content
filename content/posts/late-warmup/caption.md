If you're short on time, putting beats hitting balls. Every time.

A rushed bucket gets you nothing. You hit twenty balls badly, build no rhythm, and arrive on the first tee with fresh doubt. Meanwhile the part of your game that decides your score hasn't been touched.

Ten minutes, in this order:
→ 6 putts from three feet, to feel the strike
→ 6 long putts, to feel the speed
→ 6 chips from off the green
→ 10 slow swings with a wedge, no ball

You won't be warm. You will be calibrated, which matters more.
