A bad shot costs you one stroke. Staying angry about it costs you three.

You're allowed to be furious — that's golf. But give it a boundary. Ten seconds from the moment the ball lands, you let it go and walk. Not because you're calm, but because the next shot is the only one you can still do something about.

Some players pick a marker on the course: by the time I reach that tree, it's finished. Find yours.

The scorecard doesn't record how unfairly the ball bounced.
