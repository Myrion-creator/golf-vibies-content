The first tee is the worst possible place to use your least reliable club.

You're cold, you're watched, and your pulse is up. That's the moment most people pull the driver, because that's what you do on the first tee. Then the round starts with a provisional ball and a walk of shame.

Hit whatever you trust. A hybrid in the fairway is a better start than a driver in the trees, and nobody on that tee will remember your club by the second green.

Start the round in play. Be brave on the fourth hole instead.
