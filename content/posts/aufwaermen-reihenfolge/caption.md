Most people reach for the driver first on the range. Exactly backwards.

The driver is the longest club, the flattest swing and the biggest amplifier of every fault you have. Swing it cold for ten minutes and you'll groove a compensation you then carry for eighteen holes.

Build it the other way around:
→ 10 balls, pitching wedge, half swings, rhythm only
→ 10 balls, 7-iron, normal swing
→ 5 balls, hybrid or fairway wood
→ 5 balls, driver
→ Finish with three long putts, ignoring whether they drop

Twenty minutes, about 30 balls. This isn't practice. You aren't trying to improve anything — you're trying to arrive at the first tee already working.
